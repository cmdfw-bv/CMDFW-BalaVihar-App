**RoleBadge** — codes a persona by its role hue, with an optional mono scope label. Use on anything role-specific; the role hues are data-coding only, never page or action colors.

```jsx
<RoleBadge role="teacher" scope="JR·A" />
<RoleBadge role="bv" scope="BRAMPTON" />
```

Roles: `student`, `parent`, `teacher`, `coordinator`, `bv` (BV Coordinator), `admin`. `scope` adds a mono-set, hairline-divided scope chip.
