**Logo** — the OM mark paired with the serif wordmark and uppercase tagline. The mark is the saffron OM from `assets/chinmaya-om.png`.

```jsx
<Logo size={40} />
<Logo size={56} onDark tagline="Events & Satsangs · est. 1982" />
```

Set `wordmark={false}` for the mark alone, `onDark` on indigo/ink surfaces. Point `src` at wherever the png sits relative to your file.
