**Card** — the standard surface container: surface fill, hairline border, the signature 18px radius, soft warm shadow. Reach for it for any grouped content.

```jsx
<Card tone="surface" interactive>
  <h3>Hanuman Chalisa Havan</h3>
</Card>
```

Tones: `surface` (default), `cream` (raised cream well / banner), `indigo` (dark poster surface — text flips to cream). Add `interactive` for the lift-and-terracotta-border hover used by event cards and pickers. `padding` and `as` are passthrough.
